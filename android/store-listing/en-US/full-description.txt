StackUp Hold'em Academy is an educational app for players who want to learn poker and improve their decision-making.

Study fundamentals, terminology, strategic concepts and poker math through a structured learning path. Then put the theory into practice with quizzes, exercises and simulators designed to develop better in-game decisions.

FEATURES
• Progressive learning paths
• Fundamentals and terminology
• Applied poker mathematics
• Interactive quizzes and exercises
• Practical simulators and training scenarios
• Personal progress tracking
• Portuguese and English interface

Academy is focused exclusively on education and training. The app does not provide real-money wagering, deposits, withdrawals, cash prizes or gambling services.

Train. Understand. Improve your poker decisions.
